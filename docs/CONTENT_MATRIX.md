## Voice rules

- Use operational outcomes before platform jargon.
- Prefer concrete nouns: request, record, approval, run, file, workflow.
- Do not use unsupported counts, percentages, superlatives, setup times, certification claims, or customer proof.
- Label illustrative scenarios and previews in the visible interface.
