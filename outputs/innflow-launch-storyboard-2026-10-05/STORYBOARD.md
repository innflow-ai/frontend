# From request to done.

Original Innflow launch storyboard.

[Figma storyboard](https://www.figma.com/design/JkJnW5Q1AIVAqgoV2AqAyb/CMS-Template?node-id=1961-1398)

14 editable 1920 × 1080 frames. Proposed 50-second single-pass cut, plus a two-second loop return, matching seam, and reduced-motion still. UI examples are illustrative.

One gray-header component carries a request through context, planning, connected tools, human approval, and completion. The panel expands downward while its width, anchor, and typography remain fixed.

## The hook · 00:00–00:03

**On screen:** Work starts / with a request.

Reveal “Work starts” first. “with a request.” rises into the same type block. Keep the stage uncluttered.

TEXT BRIDGE: “a request” becomes the label on the incoming request panel. 450 ms position match; no glyph stretching.

**Sound:** A soft opening pulse. No voiceover required.

## The request · 00:03–00:07

**On screen:** “Can you take it from here?”

The compact panel settles at x 340, y 310. The request reads “Onboard Cedar by Friday.” Hold for two seconds.

TEXT BRIDGE: “from here” clears. Innflow appears as the answer; retain the request identity for the return to the panel.

**Sound:** One quiet intake click.

## Meet Innflow · 00:07–00:10

**On screen:** Meet Innflow.

A clean brand reveal on signal blue. The supplied vector wordmark is the focal point. Supporting line: “AI agents. Real workflows.”

TEXT BRIDGE: “Real workflows.” changes to “Start with the full picture.” Blue recedes into the next scene’s gradient.

**Sound:** The musical pulse opens up.

## Context arrives · 00:10–00:12

**On screen:** Start with the full picture.

Return to the same panel anchor. The shell extends downward from 360 to 648 px. Skeletons reserve the exact three-row text slots.

Keep every edge and text slot still. Fade skeletons out as customer, owner, and deadline resolve in place.

**Sound:** A restrained rising texture, no loading chime.

## Context resolved · 00:12–00:16

**On screen:** Start with the full picture.

Customer: Cedar. Owner: Maya Chen. Due: Friday. Read top to bottom; hold the fully readable state before moving on.

TEXT BRIDGE: “full picture” gives way to “next steps.” Retain the panel header, request title, width, and anchor.

**Sound:** Three soft ticks, one for each resolved row.

## Intent becomes a workflow · 00:16–00:22

**On screen:** Turn intent into next steps.

The same rows now show Prepare, Update, Coordinate. Reveal one action at a time; this is a reviewable plan, not a completed action.

TEXT BRIDGE: “next steps” becomes “Across the tools you use.” Row labels change into their destination apps.

**Sound:** A gentle rhythmic build.

## Across your tools · 00:22–00:27

**On screen:** Across the tools you use.

Native Gmail, HubSpot, and Slack artwork arrives inside the existing rows. Drafted, prepared, and ready stay explicit.

TEXT BRIDGE: “your tools” changes to “Your rules.” The action footer becomes the approval control.

**Sound:** Three light connection accents.

## The human decision · 00:27–00:33

**On screen:** Your rules. Your final say.

Pause on the approval state. Maya reviews the welcome email. A deliberate click on “Approve and continue” is required before any sent state.

A 200 ms press/release, then a 500 ms status transition. Do not show completed rows before the approval beat.

**Sound:** Music briefly makes room; one clear approval click.

## The outcome · 00:33–00:39

**On screen:** One request. Carried through.

Resolve the same rows to sent, updated, and notified. Status reads Completed. Keep the activity-trail link visible; hold for three seconds.

TEXT BRIDGE: the completed panel recedes while “Less chasing.” takes its place. No new dashboard or extra card.

**Sound:** A soft completion tone; the music resolves.

## The payoff · 00:39–00:44

**On screen:** Less chasing. / More moving.

Large, deliberate type. “Less chasing.” lands first; “More moving.” follows on the next beat. The white underline follows the second line.

TEXT BRIDGE: “More moving.” becomes “Put work in motion.” Use one left-aligned type anchor for both shots.

**Sound:** Two clean musical accents.

## The invitation · 00:44–00:50

**On screen:** Put work in motion.

Innflow wordmark, one launch line, and innflow.ai. Hold the final invitation for at least four seconds. This is the single-pass ending.

For a looping version only, continue into the return beat. For a standard launch export, end here after the hold.

**Sound:** A confident finish with a short musical tail.

Land on the exact opening composition at 00:52. Ease to rest; match the first frame’s background, text, positions, and opacity.

**Sound:** The tail clears before the opening pulse repeats.
